Who We Are

A growing company with a bigger ambition.

QBD Power Trading Contracting & Cleaning Services is a Qatar-based company founded in 2024. Our focus is practical: support professional construction environments with dependable MEP and manpower capabilities.

Within our first two years, QBD Power has worked across seven projects and alongside three main-contractor organisations. We are building our capability step by step, with the ambition to become a stronger long-term partner to Qatar's construction sector.
