What We Deliver

Capabilities built around project needs.

This page preserves the service categories present on the current QBD site. Exact scope can be refined with management before launch.

01

Mechanical Services

HVAC installation, maintenance and repair; mechanical system commissioning; industrial and commercial mechanical works; pump and equipment installation; preventive maintenance programmes; and MEP trade coordination.

02

Electrical Services

Full electrical installation; lighting systems; distribution boards and panel works; cable management and electrical dressing; fault-finding, maintenance and upgrades; and emergency electrical response.

03

Plumbing Services

Complete plumbing installation; water supply, drainage and sanitation; leak detection and pipe replacement; fixture installation and repair; hot and cold water systems; and preventive/emergency plumbing.

04

Civil Works

Structural construction and renovation; roadworks and site preparation; concrete, blockwork and masonry; tiling, plastering and finishing; structural repairs and reinforcement; and integrated project coordination.

05

Manpower Supply

Skilled and semi-skilled workforce supply; plumbers, electricians and technicians; civil workers and general helpers; short-, long-term and emergency deployment; safety-trained, site-ready personnel; and supervision by the QBD management team.

06

Cleaning & Maintenance

Post-construction deep cleaning; annual maintenance contracts; appliance installation and maintenance; scheduled maintenance programmes; commercial and residential cleaning; and rapid-response requirements.
