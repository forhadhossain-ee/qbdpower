Who We Are

Built with ambition. Growing through performance.

QBD Power Trading Contracting & Cleaning Services is a Qatar-based company founded in 2024. We position ourselves as a growing subcontracting and project-support business serving MEP and manpower requirements across Doha and Lusail.

Within our first two years, QBD Power has worked across seven projects, alongside three main-contractor organisations, with 100,000+ safely worked hours and reported revenue of 1.5M QAR.

Our Mission

Deliver dependable construction and support services with precision, accountability and professional integrity.

Our Vision

Grow into a trusted mid-tier Qatar-based contracting partner recognised for reliability, technical capability and quality.
