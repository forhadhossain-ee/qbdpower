Common Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers for main contractors, project managers and procurement teams reviewing QBD Power.

What services does QBD Power provide?
QBD Power provides MEP contracting across Plumbing, Mechanical / HVAC and Electrical scopes, supported by project-based manpower supply. We work across residential, commercial and mixed-use construction projects in Qatar.
How quickly can you mobilize a workforce?
Typical mobilization is within 5–10 business days, depending on crew size, trade type and site requirement. For urgent short-term needs, we can often move faster — share the details and we'll confirm.
What project sizes do you take on?
We support mid-tier MEP packages and workforce requirements — from private villas to multi-storey commercial and residential developments. Our focus is dependable delivery within the assigned scope, not competing for scale.
Do you work as a subcontractor or main contractor?
QBD Power operates primarily as a specialist subcontractor, supporting main-contractor organisations on MEP packages and manpower deployment. We work within the assigned scope and integrate cleanly with the main contractor's programme.
Are you registered and compliant with Qatar regulations?
Yes. QBD Power Trading Contracting & Cleaning Services is a registered Qatar-based company. We operate with valid commercial registration and follow site HSE standards required by our main-contractor partners.
How do I request a quotation?
Share your scope via the enquiry form above, email us at qbdpower@gmail.com, or call +974 5572 6596. Include project name, location, scope and timeline for a faster response.
